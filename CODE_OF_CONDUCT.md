# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. This project welcomes contributors of any background and
experience level.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing
private information, and deliberately disruptive conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate these expectations,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers listed in the repository. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled confidentially.

This document is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
